# Exact image-generation prompts

All images used the built-in `image_gen__imagegen` tool with no reference image inputs.
The two banners used `transparent_background: false`; the five category images used `transparent_background: true`.

## hero-pets.png

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: photorealistic website hero photograph, ultra-wide composition approximately 3:1. Primary request: an adorable fluffy golden retriever puppy and a small gray tabby kitten sitting cuddled side by side on a soft fluffy off-white blanket. Dog on the left of the pair and kitten on the right, both facing camera, puppy smiling with tongue out, natural expressive eyes. Scene/backdrop: softly lit clean pale powder-blue studio backdrop around #DDF2FF. Composition: animals occupy the rightmost 45% of the wide image, puppy slightly taller; keep the entire left 50% a clean empty powder-blue background for separately coded website headline. The blanket follows the bottom edge. Pets fully visible with paws and ears, photo-realistic soft fur detail, gentle daylight, fresh cheerful premium pet-shop banner aesthetic. Background can have extremely subtle out-of-focus light blue accents, no strong gradient. Constraints: no letters, no words, no logo, no watermark, no graphical UI, no button, no artificial product packaging. This is only a background photograph asset, not a website mockup.
